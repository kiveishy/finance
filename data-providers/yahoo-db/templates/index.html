About the update

An update fetches the current NASDAQ, NYSE and NYSE Arca ticker lists, downloads the full history of new tickers, adds the latest days to existing ones, and checks every ticker's history for stock splits. It runs automatically every day at the time set under Schedule, and can be started here at any time.
